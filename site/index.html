stash is a Discord bot. Post links in #read-later and it sends a couple back twice a week, so they actually get read.

How it works

  1. Post a link in #read-later.stash saves it and reacts 🔖. Already saved? You get 🔁.
  2. A notes thread opens.Named after the page, with its reading time. Anything you write there is kept as a note.
  3. Picks arrive Wednesday and Sunday.Two links you saved, favouring older and shorter reads. Change the days with /schedule.
  4. React to keep it tidy.✅ marks it read, 👍🏿 marks it loved. Links ignored three times are archived for you.

Two ways to use it

  • Personal.Your own queue. Each person in the server gets their own picks.
  • Shared.One pile for your friends. Each drop is one set of picks for everyone, with who saved each link.

Switch any time with /setup mode. Personal is the default.

Commands

/picksTwo picks from your saved links, right now.
/randomOne random unread link.
/scheduleSee or change your drop days, time and timezone.
/setupPersonal or shared mode, and where alerts go if a drop fails.
/setkeyOptional: bring your own AI key (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek or OpenRouter) to tag links by topic.

Tagging is optional and uses your own key, so you only pay for what you use. Keys are stored encrypted.
