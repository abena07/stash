a little home for links you want to read

stash is a small discord bot that saves links for later and brings two back every wednesday and sunday. because saving something shouldn't be the same as forgetting it.

add to discord how it works

here's how it works

  1. send stash a link.post it in #read-later. stash saves it with 🔖. if it's already there, you'll get 🔁.
  2. keep your thoughts nearby.every link gets its own thread, named after the page and showing its reading time. anything you write there stays as a note.
  3. get a little reminder.every wednesday and sunday, stash picks two links from your queue, leaning towards older and shorter reads. change the days with /schedule.
  4. keep track as you go.react with ✅ when you've read something or 👍🏿 when you love it. links sent three times without a read are archived.

find your kind of stash

  • personal.a quiet little queue of your own. your picks come only from the links you've saved.
  • shared.one shared pile for your server. everyone gets the same picks, and each link shows who saved it.

switch any time with /setup mode. personal is the default.

a few commands

/picksget two links from your queue whenever you want.
/randomget one random unread link.
/schedulesee or change your drop days, time and timezone.
/setupchoose personal or shared mode, and where alerts go if a drop fails.
/exportget a private zip of your read links, ready for your notes app or a backup.
/setkeyadd your own ai key (claude, openai, gemini, deepseek or openrouter) to tag links by topic. optional, of course.

tagging uses your own key. you pay your provider directly for what you use, and keys are stored encrypted.

your stash, in obsidian

  1. connect your stash.run /connect obsidian in discord to get your private code.
  2. install the plugin.install the stash plugin in obsidian and paste your code.
  3. keep everything in sync.your stash folder updates when obsidian opens and every 30 minutes.
  4. in more than one server?add each one in the plugin's settings with its own code. every server gets its own folder, so links never mix.

your notes stay yours. stash never overwrites a note you've edited or brings back one you've deleted. changed your mind? /disconnect obsidian turns it off.
